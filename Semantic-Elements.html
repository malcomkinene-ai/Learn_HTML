What Is Semantic HTML?

Published January 15, 2025

Semantic HTML uses elements that describe the meaning and purpose of their content, rather than its appearance.

Why Use Semantic Elements?

  • They give browsers and assistive technologies clearer structure.
  • They make source code easier for people to read and maintain.
  • They help search engines understand the organization of a page.
See another semantic element

<address> represents contact information for a person or organization.

Quick Tip

Choose an element for what its content means, not for how it looks.
